Product Requirements Document: GRANTLY

The Problem: Scholarship discovery and management is fragmented, overwhelming, and requires navigating poorly designed government or institutional websites.

The Solution: A seamless, single-page interactive dashboard where students can discover, track, and apply for financial assistance without friction.

Core Features (MVP for 6 Hours):

1.Discovery Feed: A highly visual, fast-scrolling feed of scholarships dynamically matched to the user's profile.

2.One-Click Tracker: A Kanban-style board (Saved, In Progress, Submitted) for instant status management.

3.Smart Filters: Client-side filtering by deadline, amount, and eligibility with zero page reloads.
